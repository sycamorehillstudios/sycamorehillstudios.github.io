Idle Painter: Delete Your Data

Idle Painter: Pixel Gallery by Sycamore Hill Studios LLC · Effective 2026-11-01

Idle Painter has no accounts of its own and we run no servers, so your data is on your device and, if you use Google Play Games, in your Google account. You can delete all of it yourself, at any time, without contacting us.

Delete your data from the game

  1. Open Idle Painter and tap Settings, then Privacy, then Delete my data.
  2. Google Play Games: while signed in, tap Delete cloud save to remove the copy of your saved game (with its cover image and purchase records) from your Play Games account.
  3. On this device: tap Reset progress to erase your studio and progress. Purchase records stay on the device so that you keep what you bought; uninstalling the game removes them too.
  4. Ads: in Settings → Privacy, Privacy options changes your advertising consent and Rewarded ads turns ads off.

If the game is no longer installed

  • Data on the device: uninstalling the game deletes it. An Android backup of the game, if Android backup is on, can be deleted in your Google account (Google Drive → Backups).
  • Play Games data (cloud save, achievements, leaderboard scores): open the Google Play Games app, go to your profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data, and delete the data of Idle Painter, or manage it at myaccount.google.com.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

What is deleted and what is kept

Each step above deletes its data immediately. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases (order history) under Google's terms; your purchases can be restored from it after a reinstall. If you emailed us, we delete your messages within 12 months; to have them deleted sooner, write to sycamorehillstudios@outlook.com.
