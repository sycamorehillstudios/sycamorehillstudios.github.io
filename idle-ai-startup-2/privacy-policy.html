Idle AI Startup 2 - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Overview

Idle AI Startup 2 ("the App") is a free-to-play idle game for Android and for iPhone and iPad (iOS), published by Sycamore Hill Studios ("we", "us"). This privacy policy explains what data the App handles and how it is used. See also our Terms of Service.

In short: the App has no user accounts of its own, we do not operate any server that receives your data, and we do not sell your personal information. The only services the App talks to are your platform's store for purchases and subscriptions, Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads, our website for a small settings file, and, if you sign in to them, Google Play Games Services on Android or Apple Game Center on iOS for achievements and leaderboards.

Data We Collect

Game Progress: Your game state (offices, upgrades, items, settings, purchases and your membership status) is saved locally on your device. We never receive it. If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include the App's saved game in your device's backup to your Google account, which Google stores under its own privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Purchases and Subscriptions: In-app purchases, including the one-time Supporter Packs, and the Pro Membership subscription are processed by Google Play on Android and by the Apple App Store on iOS. We never receive your payment details. The App receives a confirmation from the store so it can deliver what you bought, and records your purchases in your game save on your device. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Google Play or the App Store; you can cancel at any time in your store account. The App keeps the membership status in your save on your device. Purchase records are kept by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

Notifications: If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders (for example, when your offline earnings are ready) locally on your device. No push notification service, notification token or server is involved.

Advertising: The App offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob: you choose whether to watch a short video in exchange for an in-game bonus, and the App shows no other ads. To serve and measure these ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data, and may use them for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before any ad is requested. On iOS, the App asks for your permission before using your device's advertising identifier, and you can change that choice at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Players who buy the Ad Blocker are not shown video ads. The Pro Membership does not include the Ad Blocker.

Analytics: We do not use an analytics service of our own. Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob collect analytics and diagnostics for Google as described in their policies. The gameplay statistics the App keeps are stored on your device only, and we do not track your usage patterns, location, or browsing activity.

Remote settings file: At launch the App downloads a small settings file from our website (hosted by GitHub Pages) that can switch features off; GitHub may log your IP address as part of serving it. The file contains no personal data and nothing is sent to us.

Age signals: In the United States the App may ask Google Play for an age range where the law requires it; the App uses it only to comply with those laws, keeps it only while the App is open and does not store or share it.

Emails to us: If you use "Report a problem" in the App's Settings, your email app opens a message to us that already contains the App's version and language. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Google Play Games and Game Center (optional)

Signing in is optional, and every part of the game works without it. If you have a Google Play Games profile, Google may sign you in to it when the App starts and show its own small welcome banner; the App never asks you to create a profile at launch. On Android, the App also includes Google's Play Games Services software, which may collect diagnostic data for Google even if you do not sign in.

If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), the service receives your player ID and your gamertag or player name, and the App sends it:

  • the achievements you unlock and your progress toward them;
  • your scores on two all-time leaderboards (your highest office and your Founder level);
  • your weekly league score and a short code for the trophy, name frame and member badge you have (a member badge shows an active Pro Membership).

The App shows the leaderboards (other players' public names, scores and those codes) as the service provides them. Your player profile, name, achievements, scores and visibility are managed by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies (Google, Apple), together with the diagnostics they collect; you can make your profile private or remove your data in the Play Games or Game Center settings. We do not receive any of it. To tell accounts apart, the App keeps a one-way code (a hash) of your player ID on your device only. If you do not sign in, the leagues work on your device only.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except the data the services above collect. The third-party services used are: on Android, Google Play Billing (for purchases and subscriptions), Google AdMob (for optional rewarded ads), Google Play Games Services (for optional sign-in, achievements and leaderboards) and Google Play's age signals (in the United States, where the law requires it), governed by Google's privacy policy; on iOS, the App Store (for purchases and subscriptions) and Game Center (for optional leaderboards), governed by Apple's privacy policy, and Google AdMob (for optional rewarded ads), governed by Google's privacy policy; and GitHub Pages, which serves the settings file under GitHub's privacy statement. These providers protect your data under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Data Retention

We hold no copy of your game save and no account for you. Achievements, scores and Play Games or Game Center profiles are kept by Google or Apple under their policies, as is the data collected by Google AdMob and the Play Games Services software. An age range from Google Play is never stored. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you, or until you ask us to delete them.

How to Delete Your Data

  • Your game save: uninstall the App, or clear its data in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Apps > Idle AI Startup 2 > Storage > Clear storage). This deletes your progress, membership status and the player-ID hash on the device; it cannot be undone. Your purchases and subscription stay with your store account and can be restored.
  • Your device backup: if Android backup is turned on, you can delete your device's backups from your Google account (Google Drive or Google One > Backups).
  • Your Play Games profile: in the Google Play Games app (or Google Play Games settings), you can make your profile private, delete the data of this game, or delete your Play Games profile; this removes your achievements and leaderboard scores. On iOS, use Game Center in the Settings app.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads), and manage or delete the data Google holds for ads in My Ad Center and your Google Account.
  • Emails to us: write to us and we will delete them.

Because we do not run accounts or servers for the App, there is no other copy of your data for us to delete.

Security

Any data sent to the store, to Google AdMob, to Google Play Games Services or Game Center, or by the settings file download is transferred over an encrypted connection.
