Idle AI Startup 2 - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Overview

Idle AI Startup 2 ("the App") is a free-to-play idle game for Android and for iPhone and iPad (iOS), published by Sycamore Hill Studios ("we", "us"). This privacy policy explains what data the App handles and how it is used.

In short: the App has no user accounts, we do not operate any server that receives your data, and we do not sell your personal information. The only services the App talks to are your platform's store for purchases and Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads.

Data We Collect

Game Progress: Your game state (offices, upgrades, items, settings and purchases) is saved locally on your device. We never receive it.

Purchases: In-app purchases are processed by Google Play on Android and by the Apple App Store on iOS. We never receive your payment details. The App receives a confirmation from the store so it can deliver what you bought, and records your purchases in your game save on your device. Purchase records are kept by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

Notifications: If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders (for example, when your offline earnings are ready) locally on your device. No push notification service, notification token or server is involved.

Advertising: The App offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob: you choose whether to watch a short video in exchange for an in-game bonus, and the App shows no other ads. To serve and measure these ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data, and may use them for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before any ad is requested. On iOS, the App asks for your permission before using your device's advertising identifier, and you can change that choice at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Players who buy the Ad Blocker are not shown video ads.

Analytics: The App does not use any analytics SDKs. Gameplay statistics are kept on your device only. We do not track your usage patterns, location, or browsing activity.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except the data Google AdMob collects to serve the optional rewarded ads described above. The only third-party services used are: on Android, Google Play Billing (for purchases) and Google AdMob (for optional rewarded ads), governed by Google's privacy policy; on iOS, the App Store (for purchases), governed by Apple's privacy policy, and Google AdMob (for optional rewarded ads), governed by Google's privacy policy. These providers protect your data under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game save data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the App or by clearing the App's data in your device settings. We hold no copy of it. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained by Google under its own policies; you can manage it through your Google account and your device's ad settings.

Security

Any data sent to the store or to Google AdMob is transferred over an encrypted connection.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
