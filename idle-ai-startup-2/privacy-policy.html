Idle AI Startup 2 - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Overview

Idle AI Startup 2 ("the App") is a free-to-play idle game for Android and for iPhone and iPad (iOS), published by Sycamore Hill Studios ("we", "us"). This privacy policy explains what data the App handles and how it is used. See also our Terms of Service.

In short: the App has no user accounts, we do not operate any server that receives your data, and we do not sell your personal information. The services the App talks to are your platform's store for purchases, Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads, and our website for a small settings file. On Android the App also includes the Google Play Games Services software, which this version does not use for sign-in (see below).

Data We Collect

Game Progress: Your game state (offices, upgrades, items, settings and purchases) is saved locally on your device. We never receive it. If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include the App's saved game in your device's backup to your Google account, which Google stores under its own privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Purchases: In-app purchases are processed by Google Play on Android and by the Apple App Store on iOS. We never receive your payment details. The App receives a confirmation from the store so it can deliver what you bought, and records your purchases in your game save on your device. Purchase records are kept by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

Notifications: If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders (for example, when your offline earnings are ready) locally on your device. No push notification service, notification token or server is involved.

Advertising: The App offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob: you choose whether to watch a short video in exchange for an in-game bonus, and the App shows no other ads. To serve and measure these ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data, and may use them for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before any ad is requested. On iOS, the App asks for your permission before using your device's advertising identifier, and you can change that choice at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Players who buy the Ad Blocker are not shown video ads.

Google Play Games Services: The Android app includes Google's Play Games Services software development kit (SDK) so that Play Games features can be added in a later update. This version does not offer Play Games sign-in, achievements or leaderboards, and it does not send your game progress to Play Games. The SDK starts with the App and may collect diagnostic and usage data for Google, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics: We do not use an analytics service of our own. Google AdMob and the Google Play Games Services SDK may collect analytics and diagnostics for Google as described in their policies. The gameplay statistics the App keeps are stored on your device only, and we do not track your usage patterns, location, or browsing activity.

Remote settings file: At launch the App downloads a small settings file from our website (hosted by GitHub Pages) that can switch features off, for example if a problem is found after a release. GitHub may log your IP address as part of serving it. The file contains no personal data and nothing is sent to us.

Emails to us: If you use "Report a problem" in the App's Settings, your email app opens a message to us that already contains the App's version and language. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except the data Google collects through the services described above. The third-party services used are: on Android, Google Play Billing (for purchases), Google AdMob (for optional rewarded ads) and the Google Play Games Services SDK, governed by Google's privacy policy; on iOS, the App Store (for purchases), governed by Apple's privacy policy, and Google AdMob (for optional rewarded ads), governed by Google's privacy policy; and GitHub Pages, which serves the settings file under GitHub's privacy statement. These providers protect your data under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Data Retention

We hold no copy of your game save and no account for you. Data collected by Google AdMob and the Google Play Games Services SDK is retained by Google under its own policies. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you, or until you ask us to delete them.

How to Delete Your Data

  • Your game save: uninstall the App, or clear its data in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Apps > Idle AI Startup 2 > Storage > Clear storage). This deletes your progress on the device; it cannot be undone.
  • Your device backup: if Android backup is turned on, you can delete your device's backups from your Google account (Google Drive or Google One > Backups).
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads), and manage or delete the data Google holds for ads in My Ad Center and your Google Account.
  • Emails to us: write to us and we will delete them.

Because we do not run accounts or servers for the App, there is no other copy of your data for us to delete.

Security

Any data sent to the store, to Google AdMob, to Google Play Games Services or by the settings file download is transferred over an encrypted connection.
